# KnowGrove Support

KnowGrove is a local voice recorder for iPhone with an Apple Watch companion. It provides on-device transcription and organization, plus Markdown and audio exports to folders you choose.

For support or privacy questions, email [lufieu@gmail.com](mailto:lufieu@gmail.com). For non-sensitive bug reports, you can also open a public issue at:

https://github.com/lufie/KnowGrove-iOS-Support/issues

Please include the KnowGrove version, iOS version, device model, and a short description of the problem. For Watch issues, include the watchOS version and whether the paired iPhone is reachable. Do not attach private recordings, sensitive captured content, or unnecessary personal information.

Transcription and summaries can contain errors; check important details against the original recording. Apple Watch recordings transfer to the paired iPhone, where transcription and organization run. User-selected iCloud Drive or other file-provider folders may synchronize exports outside the device.

# 言续支持

言续是一款配有 Apple Watch 伴侣 App 的 iPhone 本地录音工具，提供设备端转录和整理，并支持将 Markdown 和音频导出到您选择的文件夹。

如需支持或咨询隐私问题，请发送邮件至 [lufieu@gmail.com](mailto:lufieu@gmail.com)。不含敏感信息的故障报告，也可以在以下地址提交公开问题：

https://github.com/lufie/KnowGrove-iOS-Support/issues

请附上言续版本、iOS 版本、设备型号和问题简述。Watch 问题请另附 watchOS 版本和配对 iPhone 是否可以连接。请勿附上私人录音、敏感的收集内容或不必要的个人信息。

转录和摘要可能包含错误，请与原始录音核对重要信息。Apple Watch 录音会传输到配对 iPhone，再在 iPhone 上进行转录和整理。用户选择的 iCloud Drive 或其他文件提供商文件夹，可能将导出文件同步到设备之外。
